About MicroThinkers (micro thinkers)

A team built to finish what it starts.

MicroThinkers (micro thinkers) began in 2022 when a software engineer, tired of watching client projects stall between the pitch deck and the working product, decided to run things differently. Today we're a team of more than five — full-stack developers, ML engineers, product designers, and a DevOps lead — spread across three time zones and united by one rule: the person who scopes the work is the person who builds it.

We don't run a bench of juniors learning on a client's budget, and we don't route requests through account managers who relay messages instead of answering them. Every engagement gets a small, senior pod — usually two to four people — who stay on the project from kickoff to launch and beyond.

Since our first contract, microthinkers has shipped production software for logistics firms, D2C brands, fintech startups, and internal tools teams across USA, Canada, Europe, the Gulf, and Asia — which is also why our processes are built around async handoffs and overlapping working hours, not everyone sitting in the same office.

Our mission

Give teams of any size access to senior product, engineering, and AI talent without the overhead — or the twelve-month hiring cycle — of building that team in-house.

How we work

Two-week, fixed-scope sprints, each ending in a working demo rather than a status slide. You see the product change every ten business days, and the invoice never surprises you.

Why clients stay

We inherit context once and keep it. A client on their third engagement with us gets a team that already knows their codebase, their users, their compliance constraints, and the last three decisions that didn't work.

Who we work best with

Founders past the prototype stage, in-house teams that need a senior hand for one hard quarter, and agencies who'd rather subcontract engineering than hire for a single project.
